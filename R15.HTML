R15 V4The Yamaha R15 V4 is a premium 155cc entry-level sports bike,
featuring a 18.4 PS liquid-cooled engine with Variable Valve Actuation (VVA)
for enhanced performance. It boasts advanced features like USD front forks,
traction control, a quick shifter (higher variants), and a 6-speed gearbox, with a sporty,
R7-inspired design. The R15 V4 delivers 45 kmpl mileage,,
making it a powerful, fuel-efficient choice, though it has a committed riding posture
