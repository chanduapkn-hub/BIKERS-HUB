BAJAJ PULSAR

ABOUT BAJAJ PULSARThe Bajaj Pulsar is a dominant Indian sports-commuter
motorcycle brand launched in 2001, defining performance biking with models
ranging from 125cc to 373cc. Known for its muscular design, it features
DTS-i technology, digital consoles, and, in many models, standard ABS.
The lineup includes the commuter-friendly 125/150, sporty N160/NS200, and fully-faired RS200
